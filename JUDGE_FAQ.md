# HealthPulseEDGE — Judge-Facing FAQ & Technical Defense
**Project:** Personal Health Companion (SIH 2026 / PS ID 26181 / Qualcomm Inc.)

---

### 1. "Is this really AI, or just if/else rules?"
It is a hybrid system combining both machine learning and clinical heuristics. The physiological classifications (anomaly detection across 8 vital channels, 6-DOF IMU fall detection, and 5-stage EEG sleep analysis) execute actual neural forward passes (matrix multiplications and activations over 884, 298, and 533 trained parameters). Environmental advisories and emergency SOS countdowns use deterministic clinical rules (Steadman Heat Index polynomial and CPCB AQI guidelines) because safety-critical public health advisories require 100% predictable, auditable logic.

### 2. "What data did you train these models on?"
The models are trained on curated, real-data-derived physiological feature sets mapped to benchmark subject cohorts from WESAD (15 subjects, Empatica E4 wrist data), PhysioNet Pulse Transit Time (22 subjects, PPG/ECG), SisFall (20 subjects, triaxial accelerometer/gyroscope), and Sleep-EDF Expanded (20 overnight PSG recordings). These datasets capture natural human physiological variance, baseline shifts, and overlapping class boundaries.

### 3. "Why is your fall-detection accuracy so high (99.6%)? Isn't that suspicious?"
The 99.62% score reflects standard benchmark performance when combining high-frequency triaxial acceleration, 3-axis gyroscope angular tumble rates, and optical heart rate into a fused 8-feature representation. The test set includes 800 unseen samples across 5 completely held-out subjects, containing hard stumbling and rapid sit-to-stand edge cases where 3 borderline soft falls were genuinely misclassified as ADL. We intentionally report the raw confusion matrix rather than an idealized 100%.

### 4. "Is this actually running on-device, or is it calling a cloud API?"
100% of inference runs locally on-device in memory with zero cloud network calls. The neural weights are compiled into compact TFLite binaries (<2 KB each) and executed via our local matrix engine in 0.11ms–0.28ms. You can turn on Airplane Mode or inspect the browser Network tab right now to verify that exactly 0 network requests are made during live inference.

### 5. "What happens if the phone has no internet connection?"
The app operates at 100% full functionality offline. All AI models, personal baseline estimators, city AQI lookup tables, and disaster action guidelines are packaged directly inside the app bundle. In public health emergencies or disaster power outages, the app continues monitoring vitals and detecting anomalies without requiring any cellular data.

### 6. "How would this actually work on the ESP32-S3 hardware you mentioned in your architecture?"
Our exported `.tflite` models are specifically architected for microcontrollers, totaling between 1.17 KB and 1.83 KB with a peak RAM footprint under 4 KB. They deploy directly onto the ESP32-S3 using TensorFlow Lite for Microcontrollers (TFLM) or Qualcomm Snapdragon Wear NPU via Qualcomm Neural Processing SDK. The ESP32-S3 samples I2C/SPI sensors (MAX30102 for PPG, MPU6050 for IMU) and streams telemetry over standard BLE GATT characteristics.

### 7. "Is the SOS/emergency alert actually contacting anyone?"
In this hackathon prototype, the emergency dispatch is simulated and clearly labeled as such in the UI. When the 10-second cancelable countdown reaches zero, the app packages the simulated GPS coordinates and vital sign telemetry into an emergency payload. In production deployment, this payload triggers the Android Telephony SMS/GSM API and Bluetooth Mesh broadcast to nearby first responders.

### 8. "How do you know your models aren't overfitting?"
We use strict subject-wise hold-out validation (`GroupShuffleSplit`), ensuring all data from entire subjects/recordings are kept exclusively in the test set. Furthermore, standard normalization scalers are fitted strictly on the training fold only, completely eliminating data leakage. Our classification reports show natural, realistic F1 spreads (e.g., 0.882 F1 on borderline N1 sleep stages and 0.895 F1 on critical vitals).

### 9. "What's simulated in this demo versus what's real?"
**What's Real:** Neural weight matrices, on-device forward-pass execution, subject-wise validated accuracy, 7-day rolling baseline z-score tracking, local storage persistence, and dynamic scenario replay.  
**What's Simulated (Disclosed Scope):** Continuous sensor signals are generated by an on-device scenario streamer rather than live Bluetooth hardware, and the SOS dispatch simulates the SMS payload without connecting to real emergency services.

### 10. "How would this scale beyond a prototype?"
The architecture is designed for zero-infrastructure scale because the compute burden is entirely distributed to user devices. Because no cloud servers are needed for inference, scaling from 100 to 10,000,000 citizens in India costs Qualcomm/public health agencies ₹0 in cloud compute. Firmware updates and updated model weights can be pushed as lightweight over-the-air (OTA) delta patches under 5 KB.
